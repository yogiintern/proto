Credit readiness for India's defence MSMEs

An AI-guided journey that understands your defence business — from iDEX prototype to purchase order to invoice — and connects you to the right capital: grants and venture capital at pre-debt stage, PO finance, guarantees, TReDS and term loans as you grow.

🏭 Who it's for

Defence manufacturing and services MSMEs — Tier-2/3 suppliers, iDEX/TDF innovators, DPSU supply chains.

🚫 Who it's not for

Trading / reselling businesses are outside this programme's scope. The eligibility check will tell you in the first 3 questions.

🔒 Your data, your consent

Purpose-specific consent, Indian data residency, no classified documents — ever. You approve everything before it's shared.
